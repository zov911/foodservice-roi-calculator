# Foodservice Software ROI Calculator: Senior Living & Healthcare

**Live demo:** https://zov911.github.io/foodservice-roi-calculator/

An interactive value-assessment tool for foodservice management software in senior living communities, long-term care and hospitals. Prospects enter facility size, staffing, food budget and the capabilities they care about, and get projected annual savings and first-year ROI.

## Features

- **4-step wizard:** facility profile → operations → capabilities → results
- **8 capability modules:** menu planning, resident dietary profiles, kitchen production, inventory & purchasing, and more, each with its own savings model
- **Savings breakdown** across labor hours, food waste, dietary compliance and dining revenue
- **ROI metrics:** net annual value, first-year ROI, payback and value per resident
- Polished editorial design that works on mobile

## Why it exists

ROI calculators are among the highest-converting B2B SaaS website assets. They pre-qualify buyers, give champions a business case to share internally, and hand sales a structured picture of the prospect before the first call.

## Tech

A single `index.html`: vanilla HTML/CSS/JS with no dependencies.

> Benchmarks are typical industry and vendor-reported ranges. Results are estimates.
